About Escapade

Designed for people who refuse to stay bored.

Escapade was born out of a simple question: "What should we do today?" Instead of endlessly scrolling through generic recommendation algorithms or tourist traps, Escapade aggregates genuine, creative local adventures shared by real explorers. Whether you want to find a quiet rooftop at sunset, explore a hidden neighborhood park, or try a secret local bakery, Escapade gives you immediate inspiration.
